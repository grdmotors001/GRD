(To be executed on non-judicial stamp paper of applicable value and notarized)

  1. That I am the purchaser of the vehicle described below, manufactured by {{ company.name if company else 'G.R.D. MOTORS' }}:
    • Model : {{ ti.product_name }}
    • Chassis No. : {{ ti.chassis_no }}
    • Motor No. : {{ ti.motor_no or '' }}
    • Colour : {{ ti.colour or '' }}
    • Invoice No. & Date : {{ ti.bill_no }} dated {{ ti.date.strftime('%d/%m/%Y') if ti.date else '' }}

  3. That I shall get the said vehicle registered with the concerned Regional Transport Office (RTO) within the time period prescribed under the Motor Vehicles Act, 1988 and the rules made thereunder.
  4. That I shall use the said vehicle strictly in accordance with the permit/registration category granted to it, and shall not use it for any purpose or route not permitted by law.
  5. That the statements made above are true and correct to the best of my knowledge and belief, and nothing material has been concealed therefrom.
